# Palm webOS Card View

An interactive card multitasking sketch inspired by classic Palm webOS.

Controls:

- Drag left/right: switch cards with inertia
- Flick a centered card upward: close it
- Drag a centered card up and sideways: reorder
- Tap the center card or gesture-area center button: open app
- In app view: tap `< CARDS`, gesture-area center, or right-click to return
- Keyboard: `Left/Right` switch, `Up` close card, `Enter` open, `Esc` back
